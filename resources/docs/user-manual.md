# SEIMS User Guide

**Supplies and Equipment Inventory Management System** — supplies and equipment inventory, borrowing, reservations, and maintenance for SPUP.

---

## Getting started

1. Open the SEIMS login page and sign in with your school email and password.
2. New accounts may need **admin approval** before you can use the system.
3. Use **Profile** (top-right menu) to update your name, email, or password.

---

## Students

### Dashboard
View your borrowing summary, recent activity, and quick links.

### Borrow equipment
1. Go to **Borrow Equipment** in the sidebar.
2. Search for items and add them to your cart (quantity and return window: **3–8 hours**).
3. Submit the request and wait for staff approval.
4. Pick up equipment when status becomes **Issued**.
5. Return items on or before the due time.

### Borrowing history
- Track request status: Pending, Approved, Issued, Returned, or Rejected.
- Cancel a **pending** request if you no longer need the item.
- Request an **extension** before the due date if you need more time (staff must approve).

### Room reservations
1. Open **Reservations** → create a booking for a laboratory room.
2. Choose date, time, and room; the system checks availability.
3. Wait for approval if required.

---

## Staff

### Inventory
- **Items**: add, edit, import, and manage equipment catalog and stock.
- **Unit tracking**: each physical unit has a **Unit ID** (e.g. `SEIMS-000131-U001`).
- Mark units as **damaged** when reported; corrective maintenance can be scheduled automatically.

### Borrowing workflow
1. Review **pending** requests in Borrowings.
2. **Approve** or **reject** with a reason.
3. **Issue** items when the borrower collects them.
4. **Return** when equipment is brought back; inspect condition and update unit status if needed.

### QR scanner
- Scan item QR codes to view details quickly.
- Enter a code manually if the scanner cannot read the label.

### Maintenance
- **Maintenance list**: scheduled and completed work orders.
- **Maintenance dashboard**: overdue, upcoming, damaged units, and high-wear items.
- Complete maintenance records when work is finished.

### Analytics
- Demand forecast, utilization, and maintenance predictions.
- Export reports when needed.

---

## Administrators

### User management
- Approve or reject new registrations.
- Create, edit, or deactivate users and assign roles (Admin, Staff, Student).
- Reset passwords when users cannot sign in.

### Reports & settings
- **Reports**: system and borrowing exports.
- **Settings**: system name and configuration.
